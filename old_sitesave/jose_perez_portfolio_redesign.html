About

Jose Luis Perez

I'm a Louisville-based commercial photographer focused on making products look the way they deserve to look — with intention, precision, and a genuine love for light.

My work lives at the intersection of technical control and creative direction. Whether it's a bottle of bourbon on a raw wood surface or a skincare product on a clean white field, every frame is built to communicate something.
